tide run is a free endless runner for iphone. one thumb, one run, one number to beat.

crates.

crates wash up on the water now. you take them like a coin, then open them back at the locker. most of them are a few doubloons. every so often one has something for the boat instead — a colour for your wake (fourteen, from ember to a proper rainbow) or a different way the beacon smashes when you go through it (twelve, from fireworks to an ink cloud). you can't buy crates, and you can't buy what's in them. you find them. the five deep-water hulls each carry their own wake shape too — a comet, a braid, a boil — so the boat you sail and the trail you leave are two separate things to want.

no accounts. no timers. no energy. an ad between runs, and one 99c purchase if you'd rather not.

progress lives on your phone. the leaderboard and 33 achievements run through game center.
