Tide
Run

Hold to jump. Smash the beacons.

Tide Run is a one-touch endless runner for iPhone. Hold to jump your boat over the rocks. The rocks with a light on them are beacons, and jumping one ends your run. Hold still and sail straight through instead.

Public beta on TestFlight

Free on TestFlight now. App Store listing coming soon.

Nine seas, seventeen boats.

Each sea has its own weather and its own light. Sailing further opens new waters, and doubloons buy new hulls. Beat your best distance, then beat your friends on the Game Center leaderboard.

Crates

Crates wash up on the water. Pick one up like a coin and open it back at the locker. Most hold a few doubloons. Some hold a new colour for your wake, fourteen in all, from ember to a full rainbow, or one of twelve new ways for a beacon to break when you sail through it, from fireworks to an ink cloud. You can't buy crates and you can't buy what's in them. The five deep-water hulls each leave their own wake shape as well, a comet, a braid, a boil, so the boat you sail and the trail you leave are separate things to chase.

No account. No timers. No energy.

Progress is saved on your phone. The leaderboard and 33 achievements run through Game Center. A short ad shows between runs, and a one-time 99c purchase removes it.
