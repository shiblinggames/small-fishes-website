Tide Run

Tide Run is a one-touch pirate endless runner for iPhone. Hold to jump.

How to Play

  1. Hold to jump. That is the whole control.
  2. Jump the rocks.
  3. Smash through the ones with a light. Some rocks carry a blinking light. Do not jump those. Hold still and smash straight through them on the water.

FAQ

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, and only there. Tide Run has no accounts and no servers of ours. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it.

How do the leaderboard and achievements work?

Through Apple Game Center, under your Game Center nickname. There is a leaderboard and 33 achievements. Apple handles that data; see our privacy policy.

What are crates, and can I buy them?

Crates wash up on the water. Take one like a coin, then open it back at the locker. Most hold a few doubloons; every so often one holds a wake colour or a beacon smash for your boat instead. Crates cannot be bought, and neither can what is in them. You find them.

Does Tide Run have ads, analytics or tracking?

Yes, there are ads. A short full-screen ad shows between runs, never during one, never right after a personal best, and not at all for your first few runs. You can remove them for good with a one-time purchase: the Remove Ads button sits under Ranks on the main screen, and also in the locker's Boats tab. There is also an optional ad on the wreck screen that doubles that run's beacon coins; that one is your choice and stays available after the purchase. Progress is still stored on your device only, and there is no account.

I paid to remove ads but they came back

Open the locker, Boats tab, and tap Restore purchases under the Remove Ads button. Purchases are tied to your Apple account, so this also works on a new phone.

How do the Ranks work?

Tap Ranks on the main screen. It opens on the global leaderboard, with the top three on the podium, your own rank, and the sailor just ahead of you. Friends is the other tab. Scores come from Game Center, so you need to be signed in under Settings > Game Center to appear.
