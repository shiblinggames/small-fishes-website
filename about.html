The History of Small Fishes

We're three siblings who had a passion for making games at home when we couldn't afford to buy all the latest and greatest. During COVID we decided to put our heads together and mash up a few ideas we had from childhood and make something that the world could enjoy.

It started with a different theme and gameplay entirely and after iteration after iteration became the Small Fishes game that you see here today.

Can you believe that the original game was called "Dynasty" and was based on ancient Chinese social structures? It's almost unrecognizable from the first state of the game, but we're thrilled how all our game time decisions have transformed into something that we're proud of — and hope you'll enjoy as well!
