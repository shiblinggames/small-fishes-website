Overview

Shibling Games ("we", "us", "our") operates shiblinggames.com and seasthebooty.com, and makes the Tide Run app for iPhone. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it. We keep things simple — we're a small team, not a data broker.

Information We Collect

  • Email address — if you sign up for our mailing list via the newsletter form on our homepage.
  • Purchase information — if you buy through our Shopify store at shiblingshop.com, your order and payment details are handled directly by Shopify. We receive order confirmation details only.
  • Game account data — if you play the Small Fishes online game at seasthebooty.com, that platform maintains its own privacy practices. Please review those separately.

Tide Run (iPhone App)

Tide Run stores all of your progress on your device only. It has no accounts, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information from the app.

The only external service is Apple Game Center, which Tide Run uses for the leaderboard and achievements under your Game Center nickname. Apple handles that data under Apple's privacy policy. Nothing else leaves your device.

Questions about Tide Run and your privacy? Email hello@shiblinggames.com.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you occasional updates about new games, products, and announcements if you subscribed.
  • To fulfil orders placed through our shop.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies. Our Shopify storefront may set cookies to manage your cart and session. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies on shiblinggames.com.

Third-Party Services

  • Shopify — processes purchases at shiblingshop.com. See Shopify's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — used to load typefaces on this site. Google may log your IP address when fonts are requested.
  • Apple Game Center — used by the Tide Run app for its leaderboard and achievements. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

You may unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time using the link in any email we send. To request deletion of your data or ask any privacy-related question, email us at hello@shiblinggames.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@shiblinggames.com.
